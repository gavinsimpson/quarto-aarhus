# AU artwork and fonts

The repository's MIT software licence does not relicense Aarhus University trademarks, artwork, or fonts.

- `mark-black.svg`, `mark-white.svg`, and `quote.svg` come from the user-supplied AU PowerPoint's EMF artwork. LibreOffice converted the vector paths; SVG viewBoxes were fitted to the original object bounds. No artwork was redrawn.
- `seal-white.svg` comes from https://cdn.au.dk/2016/assets/img/au_segl.svg. `seal-black.svg` retains the same paths in black.
- AU font binaries are supplied by the author through `aarhus.font-dir`; they are not redistributed with this extension. Generated decks embed those supplied files.


- ORCID icons are supplied by the embedded `quarto-ext/fontawesome` extension (1.3.0). Its Font Awesome assets retain their bundled licence; ORCID marks retain their [brand guidelines](https://info.orcid.org/brand-guidelines/). No AU font files are bundled.
